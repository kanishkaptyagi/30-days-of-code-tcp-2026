Performance and Hardware

The iPhone 17 Pro Max is powered by the A19 Pro chip, designed for speed, efficiency, and demanding applications like advanced gaming and high-resolution video editing. Processor: Features the A19 Pro chip with a 6-core CPU, 6-core GPU, and a 16-core Neural Engine for on-device AI tasks. Cooling System: A new vapor chamber cooling system, integrated into the aluminum unibody, helps move heat away from the A19 Pro chip for sustained high performance. Design & Durability: Boasts a heat-forged aluminum unibody design and improved durability with Ceramic Shield 2 on the front (3x better scratch resistance) and Ceramic Shield on the back (4x more crack resistant than previous models).

Camera System

The device features a professional-grade triple rear camera system and an upgraded front camera, offering advanced creative control and high-quality results. Rear Cameras: A triple 48MP Fusion camera system (Main, Ultra Wide, and Telephoto) offering a 16x total optical zoom range with up to 8x optical-quality zoom, the longest on an iPhone. Front Camera: A new 18MP Center Stage front camera that uses a square sensor to allow for flexible framing, including landscape selfies, and automatically adjusts the frame to keep everyone in the shot during video calls. Pro Video Features: Supports professional workflows including ProRes RAW, Apple Log 2, and ACES (Academy Color Encoding System) color support, along with 4K Dolby Vision video recording at up to 120 fps

Display and Battery

The iPhone 17 Pro Max has the largest display in the lineup and the best battery life ever in an iPhone. Display: A large 6.9-inch Super Retina XDR OLED display with ProMotion (120Hz adaptive refresh rate), an anti-reflective coating, and a remarkable 3000 nits peak outdoor brightness. Battery Life: Offers exceptional all-day battery life, with up to 39 hours of local video playback, according to AppleVis reviewers. Charging: Supports fast charging to get up to 50% charge in 20 minutes with a 40W adapter or higher (sold separately), as well as 25W MagSafe and Qi2 wireless charging.

Software and Connectivity

The iPhone 17 Pro Max runs on iOS 18, offering a refined user experience with enhanced security, privacy controls, and seamless integration with other Apple devices. Software Updates: Receives the latest iOS updates, including new features and performance improvements. Connectivity: Supports 5G networks, Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.3, and MagSafe for accessories. Security: Features Face ID for secure authentication and advanced privacy controls to protect user data.
